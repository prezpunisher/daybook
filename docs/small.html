The rules

  3. All 3 · Daybook's pick when the 15m EMA50, MACD and CCI all agree → hold to 2:50 pm, stop −30%.
  4. Stocks on your no-trade list are never taken.
  5. Signals at 8:45 am. The bot takes up to 4 a day in that order, one per stock, using the contract nearest the stock price that costs $0.25 or less, while the day's total stays within $100. (Before Nov 22, 2026: one a day at $0.50.)
